TestSplash demonstrates the splash-screen implementation in
PkUtils/UI/Splash, including its additional features and lifecycle behavior.

The original prototype was inspired by the CodeProject article
"A Pretty Good Splash Screen in C#" by Tom Clement:
http://www.codeproject.com/Articles/5454/A-Pretty-Good-Splash-Screen-in-C

The implementation has since been modified. For its history and design notes,
see PkUtils/UI/Splash/SplashForm.cs; for usage, see TestForm.cs.
